# Cut Through the Marketing Noise

**GearUpHiker** doesn't test gear in a lab. We do something different: we mine thousands of real user reviews across the web — Reddit, REI, Amazon, and specialty forums — to surface what actual hikers, backpackers, and climbers are saying about their gear.

No sponsored hype. No affiliate-driven fluff. Just raw, aggregated consumer intelligence.

---

## What We Dig Into

- 🎒 **Backpacks** — Comfort, durability, weight, and real-world carry performance
- ⛺ **Tents & Shelters** — Weather resistance, setup ease, and long-term reliability
- 🥾 **Footwear** — Break-in periods, traction, waterproofing, and mile-after-mile comfort
- 🧥 **Outerwear** — Breathability, warmth, packability, and real-world layering
- 🔦 **Everything Else** — Sleeping bags, stoves, trekking poles, headlamps, and more
